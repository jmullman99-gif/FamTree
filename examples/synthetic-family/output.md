## Giovanni Marchetti (c. 1870–1942)

Giovanni Marchetti was born around 1870 in Lucca, Tuscany, Italy. He
emigrated to the United States in 1895, arriving in New York aboard the
SS Werra on April 12, 1895. The ship's manifest records him as
"Giovanni Marchetti," age 25, occupation *scalpellino* (stonemason),
with his destination listed as "fratello, Brooklyn" — a brother already
living in Brooklyn whose identity remains unknown.

By the 1900 Census, Giovanni (recorded as "John Marquetti") was living
at 412 Union Street, Brooklyn, with his wife Maria and their young son
Anthony. He listed his occupation as stonemason and his immigration year
as 1895, consistent with the ship manifest.

Giovanni married **Maria Rossi** (c. 1874–1908), also from Lucca. The
date and location of their marriage is unknown — it may have occurred in
Italy before Giovanni's emigration, or in Brooklyn afterward. Maria died
on March 8, 1908, at age 33. Her obituary in the Brooklyn Daily Eagle
(March 10, 1908, p. 6) reads: "MARCHETTI — Maria, beloved wife of John,
age 33. Funeral from 412 Union St."

Giovanni never remarried. He continued living at 412 Union Street,
appearing in the 1910 and 1920 Census records as a widower and
stonemason. He died on February 3, 1942, at approximately age 71. His
obituary (Brooklyn Daily Eagle, February 5, 1942, p. 12) names his
survivors as "son Antonio, daughter Rosa (Mrs. R. Callahan)."

**Sources:** SS Werra manifest (1895); 1900/1910/1920 US Census;
Brooklyn Daily Eagle obituaries (1908, 1942).
