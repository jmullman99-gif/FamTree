# Research Brief: Giovanni Marchetti

## Subject
- Name: Giovanni Marchetti (also recorded as "John Marquetti")
- Relationship to researcher: Great-great-grandfather
- Born: ~1870, Lucca, Italy
- Died: February 1942, Brooklyn, New York (exact date uncertain)

## Known Family
- **Maria Rossi** — Giovanni's wife. Born ~1874 in Lucca. Died young
  in Brooklyn, possibly 1908. Researcher's great-great-grandmother.
- **Antonio Marchetti** — Giovanni and Maria's son. Born ~1897 in
  Brooklyn. Married an Irish woman (name unknown). Researcher's
  great-grandfather.
- **Rosa Marchetti** — Giovanni and Maria's daughter. Moved to Queens
  at some point. No other details.
- **Giovanni Carlo Marchetti** — Antonio's son, researcher's
  grandfather. "Everyone called him Charlie." Electrician in Brooklyn.
  Died 2003.

## Oral History
- "Nonno Giovanni came over on a ship when he was about 25. He was a
  stonemason — built half the churches in Brooklyn, or so he claimed."
- "Maria died young. Something with her lungs. Giovanni never
  remarried."
- "Antonio married an Irish girl and Giovanni wasn't happy about it
  at first, but they got along eventually."
- "The family name got mangled on some old papers — 'Marquetti' or
  something like that."
- "There might be family still in Lucca. Nobody kept in touch."

## Available Documents
- `marchetti.ged` — partial family tree exported from a cousin's
  Ancestry account. Three generations, 10 individuals.

## Research Goal
Trace the Marchetti line back to Italy. Find Giovanni's parents and
verify the Lucca origin. Find Maria Rossi's death record and any
information about her family.

## Initial Leads
- Giovanni's immigration ~1895: search Castle Garden / Ellis Island
  records and NARA for ship manifests
- Maria Rossi's death ~1908 in Brooklyn: search Chronicling America
  for obituary in Brooklyn newspapers (Eagle, Daily Times)
- WikiTree: search for Marchetti profiles connected to Lucca/Brooklyn
- Name variant "Marquetti": search census records under both spellings
- GEDCOM file: load and analyze for any details the researcher forgot
  to mention
